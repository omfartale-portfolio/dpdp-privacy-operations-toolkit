# Practical DPDP Privacy Operations Toolkit

**A fictional privacy-operations case study for a hypothetical Indian e-commerce business.**

The portfolio scenario uses the name Nova Consumer Tech. Nova, the sample vendor, contacts, processes, records and control descriptions are fictional. These materials do not document a real employer or client, and do not show that any company has implemented the controls or signed the sample contracts.

## What is included

### Workbooks

- [**DPDP inventory and RoPA practical workbook**](DPDP_Inventory_and_RoPA_Practical.xlsx) — a consolidated working inventory, processor and vendor registers, blank rights and incident logs, retention review, illustrative risk register and action dashboard. The original scenario tabs are retained for traceability; the consolidated register identifies assumptions that need evidence.
- [**Consent management assessment toolkit**](Consent_Management_Assessment_Toolkit.xlsx) — a reusable evidence checklist. The sample assessment stays **Not Verified** where no supporting evidence was provided; it does not claim controls are effective.
- [**Privacy notice review matrix**](Privacy_Notice_Review_Matrix.xlsx) — an illustrative clause-by-clause review against the fictional inventory, with sources and assumptions recorded in the workbook.
- [**Vendor privacy assessment questionnaire**](Vendor_Privacy_Assessment_Questionnaire.xlsx) — questions for a hypothetical marketing provider. Vendor response fields are intentionally unanswered.

### Documents

- [**Illustrative privacy notice (Word)**](documents/Illustrative_Privacy_Notice.docx) — a sample structure with contact fields replaced by obvious placeholders. It is not ready for a real customer-facing website.
- [**Privacy notice management summary (Word)**](documents/Privacy_Notice_Management_Summary.docx) — a dated fictional review and its follow-up priorities.
- [**Data breach response SOP (Word)**](documents/Data_Breach_Response_SOP.docx) — an operational simulation with placeholders, not an approved procedure for a real organization.

## Scope and limitations

The case study contains no live customer records, real vendor answers, verified control evidence, approved retention schedule or executed contracts. Blank logs are templates. Assumptions and suggested owners are not findings. The materials are for portfolio demonstration and discussion only; they are not legal advice or a compliance attestation. Confirm facts and check official legal sources before adapting any item for operational use.

The legal review files are dated 18–20 November 2026. They do not incorporate the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) (Amendment) Rules, 2026, which are scheduled to take effect on 1 January 2027. Review the [official Gazette notification](https://consumeraffairs.gov.in/public/upload/files/E%20Commerce%20Amendment%20Rules%202026_1789128659.pdf) before using or updating the e-commerce analysis.
